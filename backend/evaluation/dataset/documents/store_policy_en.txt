Acme Store Customer Policy

Refund policy
You can get a full refund within 30 days of delivery if the item is unused and in its original packaging. To request a refund, contact support with your order number. Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item. Digital goods and gift cards cannot be refunded.

Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days inside the country. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 12 dollars extra. International orders take 10 to 14 business days. Orders over 50 dollars ship free with standard shipping.

Warranty
All electronic products come with a 2-year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover water damage, drops, or damage caused by using the wrong charger. To make a warranty claim, send the order number and a photo of the defect to the support team.
