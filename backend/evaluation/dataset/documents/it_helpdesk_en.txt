Northwind IT Helpdesk Guide

Password reset
Passwords expire every 90 days. To reset a password, open the self-service portal and choose Forgot password. A reset link is sent to your work email and stays valid for 15 minutes. After 5 wrong attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes.

VPN access
The VPN is required when working outside the office. Install the Northwind VPN client from the software portal and sign in with your work account. Two-factor authentication is mandatory: approve the prompt in the authenticator app. If the VPN drops often, switch from Wi-Fi to a cable connection and restart the client.

Laptop replacement
Laptops are replaced every 4 years. A broken laptop can be swapped within 2 business days; book a time at the IT desk on the 3rd floor. Always back up your files to the company cloud drive before handing in a laptop, because the old disk is wiped.
